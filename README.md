# 💫 About Me:
Mohamad Faiz | Thb Designer<br>I started in 2020 with my YouTube channel, Two Hunter Gamer. Editing gameplay quickly showed me how much one image decides a click. That lesson pushed me to master thumbnails, logos, and banners—turning raw content into something people actually stopped to watch.<br>Today I bring the same instinct to professional branding and content design. As Thb Designer, I create visuals that earn the second look: logos, brand identities, thumbnails, and social media assets for clients worldwide. I work fluently in both Hindi and English, allowing me to connect with diverse audiences and deliver culturally relevant design.<br>My work is rooted in years of making content that performs, shaped by a user-first mindset developed inside gaming communities. I design with clarity, storytelling, and results in mind—because good design doesn’t just look right. It works.
